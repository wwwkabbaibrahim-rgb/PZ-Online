Privacy Policy

This basic privacy policy explains the information used by the PZ Online prototype.

Information we collect

Accounts use information such as email address and authentication details. Listings can include seller name, phone number, location, descriptions and photos. Messages, favourites, reports, offers and ratings are stored to provide marketplace features.

How information is used

Information is used to display listings, connect buyers and sellers, operate accounts, provide messaging, prevent abuse, process reports, and provide trust features such as ratings.

Photos

Listing and profile photos may be uploaded to Cloudinary using the marketplace's configured upload service.

Sharing

Public listing information may be visible to visitors. Your private account data and private marketplace conversations are intended to be accessible only to authorized users under the database rules.

Your choices

You can stop using the service and can request changes to information through the site owner. Because this is a prototype, additional privacy controls may be added as the platform develops.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details provided by the PZ Online site owner.
